# Ration privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026.

**Short version: everything Ration records stays in your browser, on your device. Ration has no
servers, no accounts, and no analytics. It never sends, shares, or sells your data.**

## What Ration stores

Ration keeps these things in your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`):

- The sites you choose to limit, with your opens, minutes, wait time, and daily goal.
- For each day: how many opens you used on each limited site, and how long you spent on each
  limited site.
- Your streak.

Ration records time only on the sites you limit. It does not keep a history of other sites you
visit.

## What Ration sends

Nothing. Ration makes no network requests of its own. There is no server for it to send data
to, no analytics or crash reporting, no ads, and no third-party code. Nobody, including the
developer, can see your data.

## Site access

Ration asks for no site access when you install it. When you save a site to limit, your browser
asks you to let Ration see that site. Ration uses that access for two things only:

1. To show its gate page when you open the site.
2. To tell when the site is the active tab, so it can time your opens and count your time there.

When you stop limiting a site, Ration gives its access back.

## Other permissions

- **Storage**: keeps the data listed above on your device.
- **Alarms**: ends an open on time and starts a new day at midnight.
- **Idle**: stops counting time when you step away from the computer.
- **Block content** (`declarativeNetRequest`): sends a limited site to the gate page, or blocks it
  when Ration has no access to it.
- **Favicon**: shows the limited site's own icon on the gate page, so your bookmarks keep the
  right icons.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Ration deletes everything it stored. You can also remove single sites on the Limits
page.
